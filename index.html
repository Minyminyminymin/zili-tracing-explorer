      Playback readout

      Timing only. These are the latest samples at the same elapsed time, not matched points.

      About playback

      Recorded timestamps, no speed normalisation. Gaps over 100 ms are not interpolated; the marker holds the last sample. +Z/−Z is world Z, not head-forward, and single-sample direction is noisy.

      Complete-drawing candidates

      Experimental. Candidates are unverified review excerpts, not cleaned drawings.

      How candidates are proposed and checked

      Start and end are the recorded samples nearest consecutive φ=0 crossings of the tracked curve position, in the same direction. No endpoint samples are interpolated. Z never chooses a boundary.

      Provisional XY checks: no tracking reset, no recording gap over 100 ms, at least 95 of 100 curve bins sampled, backtracking at most 10% of forward travel, at least 95% of samples within 2 cm of the reference, XY endpoint gap at most 1 cm. These thresholds are not validated and may reject real drawings.

      The 3D endpoint gap is reported separately and never used for selection. Returning within 1 cm is a tolerance, not proof of a full loop. The fixed φ=0 anchor can miss loops that start elsewhere.

      Browser export keeps the loaded columns only. The built-in pilot has no HMD columns; the offline files in outputs/complete_drawing_review keep all original fields.

      Matching criteria

      Ambiguous: another branch (≥84 reference indices, 30.24° of φ, away) lies within 0.2 cm of the best XY distance. Heuristics, not definitions of branch identity.

      All trial pairs

      Select a cell to compare that pair. Pairs share trials, so they are not independent.

      How the numbers are made, and what they do not show
      1. Recorded position. WorldX/Y/Z from the CSV, converted once to cm. Paths follow time order and are not joined across gaps over 100 ms.
      2. Remove stimulus rotation. Subtract the stimulus centre, then undo the stimulus Z-axis rotation θ at each sample. Canonical Z = WorldZ − centre Z. This is not an HMD correction.
      3. Match using XY only. Each sample maps to the nearest of 1,000 reference locations by canonical XY. Neither Z nor time selects a match. One sample can be reused at several locations.
      4. Compare drawing depth. Compare Z between the two drawings at jointly valid locations. The reference at Z=0 marks position only; it is not a target depth.

      Limits. Exploratory pilot, one participant. A 30 s trial is not necessarily one complete drawing; crossings and repeated visits affect XY correspondence. HMD-relative comparisons were computed offline from the full CSV and are not calculated here; they are coordinate re-expressions, not a head-fixed condition. The earlier 10.4% reduction came from θ-matching and is not a validated result.

      Reference curve and stimulus geometry

      Defaults come from TrefoilGenerator in the supplied project. They are assumed, not confirmed against the recording build. Changing them recalculates every correspondence and candidate.

      x(φ) = scale × (R1 cos φ + R2 cos 2φ), y(φ) = scale × (R1 sin φ − R2 sin 2φ). 1,000 points evenly spaced in φ.
