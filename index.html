        Playback readout

        Timing only. These are the latest samples at the same elapsed time, not matched points.

        About playback

        Recorded timestamps, no speed normalisation. Gaps over 100 ms are not interpolated; the marker holds the last sample. +Z/−Z is world Z, not head-forward, and single-sample direction is noisy.

        Full-turn candidates

        Experimental. Candidates are unverified excerpts, not cleaned drawings.

        How candidates are proposed and checked

        Start and end are the recorded samples nearest consecutive φ=0 crossings of the tracked curve position, in the same direction. No endpoint samples are interpolated. Z never chooses a boundary.

        Provisional XY checks: no tracking reset, no recording gap over 100 ms, at least 95 of 100 curve bins sampled, backtracking at most 10% of forward travel, at least 95% of samples within 2 cm of the reference, XY endpoint gap at most 1 cm. These thresholds are not validated and may reject real drawings.

        The 3D endpoint gap is reported separately and never used for selection. Returning within 1 cm is a tolerance, not proof of a full loop. The fixed φ=0 anchor can miss loops that start elsewhere.

        Browser export keeps the loaded columns only. The built-in pilot has no HMD columns; the offline files in outputs/complete_drawing_review keep all original fields.

        Matching criteria

        Ambiguous: another branch (≥84 reference indices, 30.24° of φ, away) lies within 0.2 cm of the best XY distance. Heuristics, not definitions of branch identity.

        Calibration

        Data

        RotatingTrace calibration CSVs from the summer build (23 June – 27 August 2026), cube trials only, for the 8 participants with initials. Each participant traced a static cube and a cube rotating about Z at 30°/s, 3 trials of 30 s each per session. Sessions are numbered per participant by date, then by the S number in the file name.

        Ground truth

        Wireframe cube centred at (0, 1, 0.65) m, edge 0.24 m (local 0.3 × scale 0.8), from the summer README. The 9 traced edges follow the polyline the game uses: front face 1–4, connecting edge 5, back face 6–9. The other three connecting edges are dimmed and not traced. The game does not log the cube's rotation angle, so for rotating trials the start angle is fitted per trial (2° grid, then 0.05°) to match the logged DistanceToCurve; rotation then advances at 30°/s.

        Distance

        For every recorded sample, the straight-line distance from the tracker position to the nearest point on any traced edge. This reproduces the game's own DistanceToCurve (FingerCursorVisualizer → CubeCalibrator.GetNearestCurveWorldPoint), which also drives the green / yellow / red cursor. The game records a sample only after the hand moves more than 1 mm, so pauses are not counted. The distance does not check edge order: being near any edge counts.

        Check against the logged value

        Across 1.13 million samples the recomputed distance differs from the logged value by a median of 0.03 mm.

        Per-trial measures

        Mean distance
        Overall accuracy. The main measure.
        Within 2 cm
        Share of samples at most 20 mm from an edge: the time the cursor showed green to the participant.
        Depth (z) variability
        Standard deviation of the z component of hand minus nearest edge point. Shows instability along the depth axis.
        Distance by edge
        Samples assigned to their nearest edge, then summarised per edge.

        Stability benchmark

        Models

        Linear mixed-effects models (statsmodels MixedLM, REML) with a random intercept for participant and for session within participant; the edge model adds one for trial. Fixed effects: rotating vs static, log₂ session order (centred), their interaction, and trial position within the session (0–2); the edge model adds depth edge 5 and back edges 6–9 against front edges 1–4. Mean distance and depth variability are log-transformed, so effects read as % change; within 2 cm is in percentage points. With 8 participants, between-person variance is estimated imprecisely. Residual checks show a long upper tail for mean distance and depth variability.

        Drawings

        1. Recorded position. WorldX/Y/Z from the CSV, converted once to cm. Paths follow time order and are not joined across gaps over 100 ms.
        2. Remove stimulus rotation. Subtract the stimulus centre, then undo the stimulus Z-axis rotation θ at each sample. Canonical Z = WorldZ − centre Z. This is not an HMD correction.
        3. Match using XY only. Each sample maps to the nearest of 1,000 reference locations by canonical XY. Neither Z nor time selects a match. One sample can be reused at several locations.
        4. Compare drawing depth. Compare Z between the two drawings at jointly valid locations. The reference at Z=0 marks position only; it is not a target depth.

        Limits. Exploratory pilot, one participant. A 30 s trial is not necessarily one complete drawing; crossings and repeated visits affect XY correspondence. HMD-relative comparisons were computed offline from the full CSV and are not calculated here; they are coordinate re-expressions, not a head-fixed condition. The earlier 10.4% reduction came from θ-matching and is not a validated result.

        Trials are numbered from 1 on this site. Trial 1 is TrialIndex 0 in the CSV; exported files keep the CSV index.

        Trefoil geometry

        Defaults come from TrefoilGenerator in the supplied project. They are assumed, not confirmed against the recording build. Changing them recalculates every drawing correspondence and candidate.

        x(φ) = scale × (R1 cos φ + R2 cos 2φ), y(φ) = scale × (R1 sin φ − R2 sin 2φ). 1,000 points evenly spaced in φ.
